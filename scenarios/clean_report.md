# Q2 Quarterly Financial Report — Summary

## Overview

Revenue for the second quarter came in at $4.2M, a 6% increase over Q1 and roughly in
line with the annual plan. Growth was driven primarily by renewals in the mid-market
segment, with new-logo bookings slightly below target due to a longer average sales
cycle in the enterprise tier.

## Expenses

Operating expenses were flat quarter over quarter at $3.1M. Headcount grew by four
hires, all within the engineering and customer-success teams, in line with the approved
hiring plan. No unplanned or one-off expenses were recorded this quarter.

## Cash Position

The company ended the quarter with $9.8M in cash and equivalents, consistent with the
prior quarter after accounting for normal operating burn. Accounts receivable remained
within the standard 30-45 day collection window.

## Outlook

For Q3, the finance team expects revenue growth to continue at a similar pace, with
a modest uptick anticipated from two enterprise deals currently in late-stage
negotiation. No changes to guidance are being proposed at this time.
